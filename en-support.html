Endgame Gym

Chess endgame training for adult improvers: technique drills against a perfect defender, endgame puzzles and spaced review, a few minutes a day.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or a position you think is wrong? In the app, use Settings › Report a problem (it fills in your app version, iPhone model and iOS version), or email joelhcode@gmail.com.

Common questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Endgame Gym and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal or trial end date.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud account, if iCloud is turned on for the app on each device and they use the same Apple ID.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Mostly no. All drills, puzzles and endgame tables are built into the app and work offline. The free daily puzzles need a brief connection once a day to unlock. A connection is also used for purchases and anonymous usage statistics.

How do I turn off sounds, vibration or reminders?

Open Settings in the app. Sound, haptics and the daily reminder each have their own switch.
