Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This policy explains what the Endgame Gym iPhone app does with your data. Endgame Gym is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”). Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. Your training progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud. We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app.

Data on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress, puzzle rating, streak and settings are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on for the app, they are also synced through Apple’s iCloud to your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. This copy lives in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy; we cannot access it.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used, for example which drills are started or finished, puzzle results, whether the daily set is completed, and when the purchase screen is shown. Events include your answers to the first-run questions (your rating range, goal and weakest endings), the app version, device model, iOS version and language. They are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email, Apple ID or any other identity. PostHog stores these events in the United States, and we configure it not to keep your IP address. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

Reminders

Daily reminders and the trial reminder are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes its data from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, turn off iCloud for Endgame Gym or delete its data in your iPhone’s iCloud settings. Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Because they are anonymous we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can.

Children

Endgame Gym is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
